Strategic Overview

Vietnamese consumers have been buying Gigabyte for decades — without knowing the name.

Gigabyte mainboards, GPUs, and laptops are inside millions of Vietnamese machines. But as a brand? Virtually invisible. The 40th anniversary is the single best opportunity to change that — and do it while competitors are running standard materials.

This isn't a campaign to celebrate 40 years. It uses 40 years of engineering credibility to introduce Gigabyte to a market that's been buying their products without knowing their name.

Event Strategy

40 small fires. Then one blaze.

Gigabyte has no community presence in Vietnam. You can't build community with one big event — you need to earn it first. The mini events do the groundwork. The festival collects the harvest.

"40 Years · 40 Destinations · 1 Journey." Each mini event is a numbered chapter in the same story. By stop 30, cities that haven't been visited are asking when it's coming to them. The Big Festival is stop #41 — the finale.

Event narrative

03
Media & Content

Content designed to be shared — not just posted.

Every content format below has a specific mechanism for why people share it. The distinction matters: content people share earns trust that content people scroll past cannot buy. Authentic reach always outperforms paid reach on high-consideration purchases.

Content Pillars

Heritage Series

40 Years in 8–10 Episodes

Short episodes (60–90s), released steadily across the campaign. Each tells one milestone: products that changed an industry, "first-ever" moments, technology nobody expected. Long-form on YouTube, cut for TikTok/Reels. Builds a viewing habit — people wait for the next episode like a series.

Inside Access

Unscripted Gigabyte Interviews

Candid format with Gigabyte team — Vietnam and ideally HQ Taiwan. Questions: "Which product are you most proud of?", "When did something almost fail?". Authenticity that no PR release can replicate. If an HQ Taiwan engineer appears — that's exclusive content no KOL can produce.

40 Tour Diary

Real-Time Activation Content

Every mini event stop published same day with stop number and city tag. Story format. Followers track the tour as it unfolds. By stop 20, cities not yet visited start asking. The Big Festival is the episode everyone's been waiting for.

User Voice

Real Users, No Script

5–8 genuine Gigabyte users: gamers, students, content creators, net café owners. One question unlocks everything: "What parts did your first build use?" — that single question triggers a generation of Vietnamese PC builders to reminisce and comment.

5 Viral Mechanics — Built-in Sharing Logic

01

"What parts did your first build use?"

Trigger: Nostalgia + Identity

Gigabyte mainboards were in Vietnamese PC builds from the 2000s. This question taps personal memory — the community tells the story, Gigabyte doesn't have to.

KOL seeds the question → community answers → Gigabyte reposts the best responses → loop runs without additional budget.
02

Challenge: Build "Powered by GIGABYTE" Under X Million

Trigger: Competition + Utility

PC builders love optimizing for budget. Gigabyte sets the problem, a KOL demonstrates first, the community follows. Best build wins a prize at the Big Festival.

Every submission = one organic post about Gigabyte. A winner creates a reason for others to join. Viral because it's both competitive and practically useful.
03

40-Day Countdown — One Surprising Fact Daily

Trigger: Habit + Discovery

40 days before the festival, one thing nobody knows about Gigabyte each day — backstory, a strange record, an unexpected design decision. Builds a daily page-check habit.

"Did you know this?" is the most natural sharing mechanic. Every post is a reason to come back tomorrow.
04

Reaction Video: "First Time Trying AORUS"

Trigger: Social Proof + Surprise

Real people who've never seen AORUS before — students, casual gamers, office laptop users — react genuinely to AORUS MASTER 16 or an AORUS OLED monitor. No script.

Watching someone be genuinely surprised triggers curiosity in viewers. Converts directly into store visit intent.
05

Meme: Relatable Gaming Moments

Trigger: Relatability + Speed

Vietnamese gaming communities share memes faster than any other content format. 2–3 contextual memes per week tied to campaign moments and trending topics in gaming culture.

Memes don't need big budgets — they need speed and tone. Must be published within 24 hours of the relevant moment.

Partnership Strategy

Every partner wants something different. Pitch accordingly.

Partnership strategy fails when every partner receives the same ask. Intel, VNG, and Phong Vũ have completely different motivations. Structure the conversation around what they gain — not what Gigabyte needs. Lead with their ROI, and the funding conversation becomes easy.

Chip Brands

NVIDIA · Intel · AMD · Microsoft

Quarterly MDF already exists — the 40th anniversary is leverage for an extraordinary top-up above standard allocation. The pitch: "This campaign sells your silicon too. Every Powered by GIGABYTE build features your processor. Every AORUS laptop showcase runs your GPU. Co-branded demo zones at the festival. Your logo in every demo build." Brands that see their products at a 5,000-person gaming festival don't need to be convinced — they need to be shown the opportunity clearly. Target: NVIDIA 150–200M · Intel 100–150M VND.

Publishers

VNG (Delta Force) · Garena (FC Online)

This is not a sponsorship ask — it's a co-organizer relationship for the tournament. VNG and Garena amplify through their own channels: in-game banners, community posts, tournament listings on their platforms. They bring their player base to Gigabyte's event; Gigabyte brings hardware credibility to their esports ecosystem. Exchange: prize pool (in-kind) and channel marketing support. No cash required — mutual reach benefit.

Universities

Bach Khoa · RMIT · FPT · HCMUS + others

Sponsor internal esports clubs and student gaming tournaments with Gigabyte product prizes. Deploy demo units for 1-day on-campus sessions. Tournament online qualifying includes a dedicated university bracket — giving student communities a direct stake in the Big Festival. This doesn't build this quarter's revenue. It builds Gigabyte's customer base for the next five years in Vietnam.

Retailers

Phong Vũ · GEARVN · An Phát · CellphoneS · Hacom

Retailers execute the campaign in their own channels with consistent "GIGABYTE 40" messaging. The forecast-linked gift bundle gives each retailer a commercial incentive to invest in promotion. Gigabyte sets the narrative and brand standards; retailers amplify. Critical: no equal co-branding at campaign level. The 40-year milestone belongs to Gigabyte. Retailer logos appear in their own materials, not on Gigabyte's campaign assets.

In-Store Presence / POSM

At the shelf, Gigabyte currently doesn't exist. That changes.

Every competitor — Lenovo, MSI, ASUS ROG, Predator — has branded in-store presence. Gigabyte has nothing. The 40th anniversary is the moment to not just match the competition, but to own the "40 years" visual while competitors are running standard brand materials.

Competitive Benchmark Predator's in-store display has the strongest gaming identity at retail — not because it's the largest, but because its 3D logo creates depth and RGB creates movement. Static flat logos disappear. An AORUS Eagle lightbox in 3D with ambient RGB will be the first Gigabyte visual that stops foot traffic. That's the entire job of the hero display unit: make someone pause who wasn't planning to.

Rule: Visible Over Described

The gift bundle must be displayed physically — laptop next to the monitor and gift items, priced together. If understanding the promotion requires reading a flyer, it won't be read.

The MSI MAG 274QF X24 27" must be unboxed and placed beside the laptop or PC build — not printed on a poster. Seeing it real creates wanting it real.

Adjustment Protocol

  • Week 2: If product content underperforms, shift editorial toward meme and viral formats — organic reach responds within 48 hours
  • Week 4: Review digital lead quality — if CPL is high, add lower-AOV products (accessories, monitors) to warm up the funnel before pushing high-ticket items
  • Post-campaign: Audit Lucky Draw database — segment by product interest, build retargeting lists for next 90 days

On High-AOV Conversion Timing

High-ticket items (laptops, flagship PCs) require consideration time. A customer who engages on Day 3 may convert on Day 20. The 45-day window is intentional.

Strategy: GVGS → generate traffic and CDP data → nurture with product content → drive in-store experience sessions → close. Never expect first-touch conversion on a 30–90M VND purchase.
