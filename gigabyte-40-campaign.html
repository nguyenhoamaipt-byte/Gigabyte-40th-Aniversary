00 / Overview

Campaign at a Glance

Objective

Cement GIGABYTE as Vietnam's #1 trusted hardware brand on its 40th anniversary — converting brand heritage into measurable sell-through and long-term customer loyalty.

Target Audience

Tech-first consumers aged 18–35: university students, gamers, creative professionals, and SMB buyers actively in the market for laptops, desktops, and components.

Core Concept — Enter Infinity

Forty years is not a finish line — it's an inflection point. "Enter Infinity" positions GIGABYTE's anniversary as the gateway to its next era: smarter hardware, deeper community, and a commitment to innovation without limits. Every campaign element — promotion mechanics, event experiences, media content — flows from this single idea.

Promotion Excellence

Value-added bundles and exclusive anniversary offers that convert intent into purchase

Brand Awareness

Sustained presence across paid, owned, and earned channels throughout Q2–Q3 2026

Sales Effectiveness

In-store execution and partner co-investment driving measurable revenue growth

Community Building

Turning customers into advocates through exclusive anniversary experiences and recognition

Mechanics That Convert

STEP 01

Qualify

Purchase eligible GIGABYTE product during campaign window

STEP 02

STEP 03

Register

Submit purchase code via microsite or QR — enter CDP ecosystem

STEP 04

Reward

Receive bundle gift + Anniversary Lucky Draw entry

Mechanic A

Laptop × GIGABYTE Gear Bundle

Purchase any GIGABYTE laptop and receive a curated GIGABYTE gaming accessory gift — headset, mouse, or backpack — reinforcing the full ecosystem play.

  • Anniversary-edition GIGABYTE accessory (GVGS line)
  • Lucky draw entry (1 per unit)
  • Extended 3-year warranty activation
  • Microsite registration unlocks digital content
Mechanic B

PC "Powered by GIGABYTE" Combo

Co-buy GIGABYTE VGA + Mainboard and unlock bundled savings — positioning GIGABYTE as the go-to full-system brand for PC builders.

  • Instant discount on qualifying VGA + MB combo
  • Free GIGABYTE thermal paste set
  • Priority tech support access (6 months)
  • Co-branded "Build Journey" social content kit
Mechanic C

Anniversary Lucky Draw

Every qualifying purchase generates one draw entry. Grand prize culminates at the Big Festival — creating sustained suspense and repeat engagement throughout the campaign.

  • Weekly draws via microsite (minor prizes)
  • Grand draw at Big Festival (major prizes)
  • CDP capture — data fuels post-campaign CRM
  • Social share mechanic multiplies organic reach
