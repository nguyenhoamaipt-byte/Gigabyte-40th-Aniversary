40
Heritage Can't Be Copied

40 years is a moat no competitor can cross.

A rival can match a spec sheet by Friday. Undercut a price overnight. Clone a campaign in a week. What they can never do — is manufacture 40 years of trust.

2026 is the only year GIGABYTE turns 40. This anniversary is non-renewable — a once-in-a-generation license to tell our story at full volume. And the Big Event is where that story becomes something people can touch, feel, and believe.

Heritage on a screen is information. Heritage in a room is conviction.

If we don't claim this moment in 2026 — then when?
