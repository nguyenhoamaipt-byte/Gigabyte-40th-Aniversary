2.2bContent Roadmap

5 Content Pillars

01
UGC · Nostalgia
"Máy tính đầu tiên của mày dùng linh kiện gì?"
GIGABYTE mainboard has been in Vietnam since the early 2000s — every first-gen PC builder has a story. This single question unlocks VOZ, Facebook PC groups, and TikTok simultaneously. No production budget needed. Community does the work. Peaks around week 3–4 of campaign launch.
02
Challenge · Viral Mechanic
Challenge: "Build Powered by GIGABYTE Infinity under X triệu"
PC builder community thrives on budget optimization challenges. KOL builds first → community follows → best build gets featured at Big Event + wins prize. Competitive + practical = shares. Directly drives Infinity tier adoption and component sales uplift.
03
Brand Story · Human
"GIGABYTE nhân viên kể chuyện" — Confessional format
Not a corporate interview. "Day in the life" or "Things I wish I knew" — GIGABYTE VN staff or HQ team shares real stories: which product they're most proud of, the time something almost failed, the product that changed everything. Authentic tone wins over press releases every time. Builds real brand trust.
04
Countdown · Daily Habit
40-day countdown — 1 GIGABYTE fact per day
Starting 40 days before Big Event. Each day: 1 unknown fact — not spec sheets, but backstory, records, odd products, historical firsts. Small format, easy to consume, builds daily follow habit. KOL shares each fact with their reaction to amplify reach without additional production cost.
05
Reaction · Authentic Proof
Reaction video: "Người chưa biết GIGABYTE thử AORUS lần đầu"
Cast non-enthusiasts — students, casual gamers, everyday users — and capture genuine first reactions to AORUS performance. No script. Real surprise converts better than any polished review. Distributed across TikTok + YouTube Shorts. Works especially well for laptop line targeting new-to-brand audience.
