## 3. Approved campaign brief (the spec)

### Global rules
- Byline on every deliverable: **"Prepared by Mai Nguyen (Mia)"** — never company/team attribution.
- All output in **English**.
- Style: dark premium + **orange `#FF6A1A`**; Space Grotesk + DM Sans; less text, more visual.
- Pitch/presentation content stays **project-focused** — no personal track-record / "why me" sections.

### Timeline — 6 months (T6–T11), 3 phases
- **IGNITE** (T6–T7) — Teasing / Warm-up. Sub-phases: Teaser, Launch.
- **RISE** (T8–T10) — Booming. Sub-phases: Awareness, Activation, Amplify.
- **INFINITY** (T11) — Peak. Sub-phase: Climax (+ Sustain / Q4 bridge).
- **Big Event is at the END (T11 / Month 6)** — the peak. (The roadmap template image showed it at month 3; we ignored that and kept the report's timeline.)

### PART 1 — Sale Promotion (two branches)
- **① Branding overall:** Lucky Draw — runs T6→T11, **monthly draws** + Grand Draw at the Big Event. Every purchase from any branch auto-enters.
- **② Drive sale (by category):**
  - **Laptop — Gift Package** (T7–T9): tiered by product. Premium models → **HQ Limited Package** (scarce, from HQ); other models → **40th Anniversary Package** (40-year logo, wide production). Contents flex by tier (not just "gear").
  - **Powered by GIGABYTE** (T8–T10): 3-tier component program shown as a **comparison matrix** — Essential (2+ parts) / Pro★ (4+) / Infinity (6+ full). Principle: "the more GIGABYTE in your build, the more you unlock." Escalation: warranty 12/18/24mo, draw 1×/2×/3×, support —/6mo/lifetime, sticker→badge→serial cert, VIP+featured only Infinity.
  - **Bundle PC & LCD** (T9–T11): two layers — (1) sales mechanic: high-end PC → free monitor, mid → buy-together voucher; (2) collectible: **AORUS Robot 40th Infinity Edition** (serial-numbered, campaign-only, given with every bundle, reviving the legendary 2018 AORUS Robot).

### PART 2 — Marketing
- Opens with **Heritage manifesto** ("40 years is a moat no competitor can cross… if we don't claim 2026, then when?") — bold/provocative.
- **3 phase × 3 track matrix** (IGNITE/RISE/INFINITY × Online / Offline / Partnership) as the overview.
- **Partnership:** orbital hub with GIGABYTE at centre + 4 nodes — Tech Brands (Intel/AMD/NVIDIA/Microsoft), Game Publishers (Garena/VNG/VTC/Riot), KOL Network, Universities — plus a value-exchange table.
- **PR & Media** (restructured into two branches):
  - **PR** = KOL (Ambassador **Hoàng Luân** SOW: be the face / lead Build Challenge / host Big Event; + Satellite Tier 2–4) **+ Press** (Tech: GameK/TechZ/VnReview · Gaming: ELLE Gaming/Game8 · Mainstream: VnExpress/Tuổi Trẻ).
  - **Media** = **Content Pillars** distributed across **Media Architecture** (Owned channels + Paid channels). Flow: created once → owned → amplified by paid.
  - **5 content pillars** (original 5, with light/subtle product weaving): 1) Nostalgia UGC "first PC parts?" 2) Build Challenge "under X mil" 3) Staff confessional 4) **AI in Action** (replaced the Countdown pillar) 5) Reaction "first AORUS try". Each has channel tags.
- **Decoration & POSM:** two layers — Brand (lightbox tower, illuminated signage) + Product (laptop stand, monitor corner strip); 4 CSS mockups; concise.
- **Event — "Road to Infinity":** 3 activations run **parallel T6→T10** — 39 Store Activations (GIGABYTE-run), Gaming Tournament (cyber cafés), University AI Workshop — cascading into the **Big Event (T11): Enter Infinity Festival, 2,000+**, split Online (spark demand) / Offline (explore brand & products).

### KPIs (illustrative — to validate)
`+25% YoY sell-through · 15M+ impressions · 10K+ CDP profiles · 5K+ UGC #EnterInfinity · 2K+ Big Event attendees · 3×+ partner co-investment`

## 4. Decision log (key decisions & rationale)

- **Candidate POV, project-focused** — the deck/report are a job-interview pitch; let the strategy prove capability, no "why me" slides. (Mia: "đừng đi sâu vào bản thân tôi, chỉ tập trung vào flow của dự án.")
- **Byline = Mai Nguyen (Mia)** on everything — durable rule for all future reports.
- **Timeline kept at Big Event = T11** even when a reference roadmap put it at month 3 — content always follows the report.
- **Phase naming IGNITE / RISE / INFINITY** (chosen over Awaken/Rise/Infinity and plain Teasing/Booming/Peak).
- **Earned media dropped as a channel** → became a KPI outcome (UGC count). Media = Owned + Paid only.
- **Content pillars: keep the original 5 + subtle product PR** (not a product-heavy rewrite); AI added by **replacing** the weak Countdown pillar.
- **Lucky Draw = monthly** (not weekly).
- **Gift bundle "given to every bundle" but campaign-only + serial** for scarcity (AORUS Robot).
- **Colour:** amber-gold → **vibrant orange `#FF6A1A`** across report + deck; pop pink/cyan at highlights; 4 track colours on the roadmap.
- **Why Now market stats removed** — the monitor-only figures (+18.4% / 71% / +19pp) were too narrow & unverified; replaced with qualitative drivers (Premium shift / AI-PC wave / 40th moment).
- **Reference admired:** the GEARVN→GIGABYTE pitch deck flow (Why Now → Challenge → Big Idea → Plan → Credibility → Ask) and a 4-track × 6-month roadmap **template** (structure only; content from our report).
